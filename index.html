Collection Centres

1. Central Civic Collection Hub

Location: Central Ward, Green Avenue

Accepts: Mobile phones, laptops, batteries, televisions

Schedule: Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–5 PM

Instructions: Back up and erase personal data. Remove SIM and memory cards.

Status: Awaiting Processing

2. Northside Resource Centre

Location: North Ward, Lake Road

Accepts: Laptops, keyboards, mice, mobile phones

Schedule: Tuesday–Sunday, 10 AM–6 PM

Instructions: Remove personal data and tell staff about damaged batteries.

Status: Under Recycling

3. East Market Drop-off Point

Location: East Ward, Market Street

Accepts: Phones, chargers, batteries, small appliances

Schedule: Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–4:30 PM

Instructions: Keep cables separate. Do not use household recycling bins.

Status: Successfully Recycled

4. South Municipal Depot

Location: South Ward, River Road

Accepts: Televisions, monitors, desktops, laptops

Schedule: Wednesday–Sunday, 9 AM–3 PM

Instructions: Transport screens upright and protect them from impact.

Status: Awaiting Processing

5. Westside Battery and Device Point

Location: West Ward, Orchard Lane

Accepts: Rechargeable batteries, phones, tablets, laptops

Schedule: Monday–Saturday, 11 AM–7 PM

Instructions: Cover exposed battery terminals. Never put batteries in general waste.

Status: Under Recycling
